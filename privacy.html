Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-18. This policy is provided in English and 中文.

English

Unitix is a set of browser-based unit converters. We designed it to be private by default:

  • No account. You never sign up or log in.
  • No uploads. Every conversion runs locally in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to our servers.
  • No analytics of your inputs. We do not record the values you convert.
  • Advertising (Google AdSense). We may show ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons and your IP address to serve and measure ads. See Google’s partner policy: how Google uses data when you use our partners’ sites.
  • Affiliate links. Some links are Amazon Associates links. If you buy through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you.

If you have questions, contact the site operator through the project owner. This site is for informational and educational use; it is not a substitute for professional advice.
